The chain

Mining that a person can still do

BLAKE2b resets the hardware race and hands template building back to miners. Three things define this network.

BLAKE2b, not SHA-256

Every block since 961,640 is hashed with BLAKE2b-256 under a 164-byte header. SHA-256 ASICs cannot mine it, so the chain is mined by new hardware, GPUs and CPUs rather than by whoever owns the largest SHA-256 farm.

One history, 21 million coins

Balances, addresses and rules are Bitcoin's, up to the fork height. The subsidy is 3.125 XBT a block, halving on Bitcoin's schedule, with the same 21 million limit and the same 2016-block retarget.

Miners build the blocks

Most of the hashrate here runs DATUM: each miner's own node chooses the transactions, and the pool only sets the payout split. Rewards are paid inside the coinbase of the block that earned them, not from a pool wallet.

Before you mine or receive XBT: this chain shares every block of history with SHA-256 Bitcoin up to 961,640, so an address holding real BTC must never be used here. Use a fresh wallet, kept only for XBT. Why this matters.

On the public stratum? Ending

The hosted stratum is being retired: work sent through it will not continue to be supported or paid. Move to your own gateway now — your accepted work follows your address, so nothing is lost in the switch.

How to move →

Why the change. A block is only honest work if the miner built it. Nodes are also deciding to invalidate coinbase payouts to addresses that hash through a pool's stratum rather than their own node — and that would apply on every pool, not just this one. Running your own gateway settles it.

    Long coinbase maturity

    Bitcoin Knots 29.4.2 holds newly mined coins for 6,480 blocks instead of 100, until block 979,920. It makes hit-and-run mining unprofitable. What it means for payouts.

  4. Block 979,920 · about 5 Nov 2026

    The rule lapses

    Coinbase maturity returns to 100 blocks. Coins mined during the window unlock as they reach 6,481 confirmations.

Running a node?

Gateway operators build blocks from their own node, so the node's rules are the ones that count. Run Bitcoin Knots 29.4.2 or later: an older node will accept blocks the network rejects, and everything it mines on top of them is wasted.

Questions

The short answers

Is this a new coin?

No. It is Bitcoin's ledger, continued under a different proof of work from block 961,640. If you held bitcoin before that block, the same balance exists on this chain at the same address. It trades as XBT to tell the two chains apart.

What can I mine it with?

Anything that can hash BLAKE2b: purpose-built ASICs, GPUs, even a CPU for testing. SHA-256 bitcoin miners cannot. See Start mining for the three ways to connect.

How do payouts work on Lazarus?

Every block Lazarus finds pays miners directly in that block's coinbase, split by accepted work in the TIDES window. There is no balance held for you, no withdrawal button, and nothing owed if the pool disappeared tonight.

Why does my payout take so long to spend?

Coinbase outputs always mature before they can be spent. Until block 979,920 that wait is 6,481 confirmations rather than 101, because of the long coinbase maturity soft fork. The coins are yours the moment the block confirms; they are only locked from spending.

I am on the public stratum today. What should I do?

Set up your own DATUM gateway and point your miners at it. Your accepted work is tied to your address, not to how you connect, so your place in the payout window carries over. The migration steps take about an evening, most of it waiting for a node to sync.

Where do I ask for help?

The Discord is the fastest way to reach miners and the people running the pool. Announcements go out on X.
