Connect a DATUM gateway

OCEAN model: your templates, our coinbase split.

Point your DATUM gateway at Lazarus — not at another pool. Your Knots node builds the block. Your ASICs talk to your gateway on its stratum port. The gateway talks to Lazarus Prime. A block found by anyone on this pool pays the share window in that block’s coinbase, minus 0.5%.

  1. Run Bitcoin Knots (BLAKE2b) and datum_gateway on your machine.
  2. Merge the JSON below into your datum_gateway_config.json datum section. Keep your own bitcoind, mining.pool_address, and stratum settings.
  3. Point each miner at your gateway (usually stratum+tcp://127.0.0.1:23334), not at Lazarus stratum.
  4. Set each miner username to bc1q…youraddress.worker. The part before the first . is who gets paid.

Payouts

      
On-chain, in the found block

    

    

      
When anyone on the pool finds a block — your gateway or ours — miners are paid in that block’s coinbase. 99.5% is split by accepted work in the current window (OCEAN / TIDES); 0.5% is the pool fee.

      
Outputs land when the block is found. They are immature until 100 confirmations, like any coinbase.

    

    

  


  

    

      
How it works

    

    

      
Lazarus runs a DATUM Prime on this chain. Remote operators point their own DATUM gateway here and keep their own templates. ASICs can still use our public stratum. Shares prove work. A found block pays miners in the generation transaction. Coinbase tag is Lazarus plus the required headline.
