DATUM gateway

Your templates. Our coinbase split.

Point your datum_gateway here. Your Knots node builds the block; ASICs talk to your gateway; the gateway talks to Lazarus Prime. A block found by anyone on the pool pays the share window in that block’s coinbase, minus 0.5%.

Setup, config, and usernames
  1. Run Bitcoin Knots (BLAKE2b) and datum_gateway.
  2. Merge the JSON into your datum_gateway_config.json datum section. Keep your own bitcoind, mining.pool_address, and stratum settings.
  3. Point each miner at your gateway (usually stratum+tcp://127.0.0.1:23334).
  4. Username bc1q…youraddress.worker — the part before the first . is who gets paid, and must be an address this chain accepts (BadUsername / reason 14 otherwise).

How it works

    

    

      
Your payout address is the record. Switching from public stratum to your own DATUM gateway, or from :23334 to :3333, does not wipe accepted work or the Prime payout window. The session number on public stratum can reset; that is not a lost share.

      
Remote operators point their DATUM gateway at Lazarus Prime and keep their own templates. ASICs use :23334. GPUs use :3333. A found block pays miners in the generation transaction. Coinbase tag is Lazarus plus the required headline.
