How it works

      
For newcomers and operators alike

    

    

      

        
1 · You hash, the pool counts

        
Point a miner at the public stratum, or at a DATUM gateway you run. Either way your username is your Bitcoin address. Every share you send is rebuilt into a full BLAKE2b header and hashed by Prime; accepted work is credited to your address in the TIDES window.

      

      

        
2 · The window decides the split

        
The window holds the last 8 × network difficulty of accepted work — days of pool work at typical hashrate, not the last hour. A found block pays each address in proportion to its share of that window, less its fee. A new rig starts near 0% and ramps as its work enters and older work ages out.

      

      

        
3 · The block pays you directly

        
Prime hands every gateway the same coinbase output list — the one shown under Next payout. A share whose coinbase pays anything else is refused, so whoever finds the block, it pays the window. There is no pool balance to withdraw and no operator to trust with your coins.
