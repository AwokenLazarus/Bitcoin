Payouts

On-chain, in the found block

When the pool finds a block, miners are paid in that block’s coinbase — same idea as Ocean. 99.5% is split by accepted difficulty this round; 0.5% is the pool fee.

Outputs land when the block is found. They are immature until 100 confirmations, like any coinbase.

How it works

Lazarus is a DATUM gateway on a BLAKE2b Knots node. Shares prove work. A found block pays miners in the generation transaction. Coinbase tag is Lazarus plus the required headline.
